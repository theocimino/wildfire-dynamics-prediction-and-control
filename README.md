# 🌲 Wildfire Modeling, Prediction, and Optimal Control
## Abstract
Wildfire propagation is a critical environmental challenge driven by complex population dynamics. This repository models the interplay between forest density growth and stochastic fire outbreaks using continuous mathematics and discrete simulations. By generating large-scale synthetic data, we train Machine Learning models to predict fire risks and validate them against rigorous analytical frameworks. Finally, we formulate the firefighting strategy of "controlled burns" as an optimal control problem, dynamically reducing fuel continuity to prevent catastrophic mega-fires.   

## 🎯 Project Goals & Architecture
This project explores the complex dynamics of forest growth and randomly generated wildfires through a hybrid approach combining applied mathematics and artificial intelligence. The workflow is built around three main pillars:

- Mathematical Modeling & Simulation (0D & 2D):
Formulating and solving ODE systems (population dynamics) and 2D Cellular Automata / Finite Difference methods to simulate vegetation growth and spatio-temporal fire spread.

- Machine Learning & Analytics:
Generating large datasets of fire propagation to train predictive ML models (e.g., CNNs) for spatial risk assessment, and comparing their performance against analytical stability results.

- Optimal Control Strategy:
Implementing preventative strategies inspired by real-world firefighting. We optimize the timing and location of controlled preventative burns to break fire propagation dynamics and protect the ecosystem.
